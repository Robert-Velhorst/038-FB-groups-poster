Made for people, not data analysts

For the small moments that build trust.

Whether you run a local business, organize a neighbourhood, or simply want to be more present in the groups you care about, Common Ground helps you make space for the conversations that matter.

See the workspace 

Built for people, with control

Your data. Your decisions.

Common Ground is designed to support thoughtful participation, not to speak for you.

Nothing is sent automatically

Posts, comments, and messages are always your choice. Review the original conversation and group rules before you join in.

Use the Actor you configure

Apify Actors vary in what they collect. Common Ground only imports results returned by the Actor you choose, and results still need your review.

Your workspace stays on your device

Groups, saved people, conversations, and drafts are stored in this browser. Export a backup or clear your data from Settings.

Only collect sources you are allowed to access. A Facebook group membership does not authorize bypassing technical access controls, and Common Ground does not verify source permissions.
